Privacy

Your prayers are your business.

Noor is built so that it does not need to know anything about you.

Calculated on your phone

Prayer times, Qibla and the Hijri date are worked out on your device from your location, the date and your chosen method. They work offline.

No ads. No account. No analytics.

No sign-up and no tracking. Noor asks for your location only while you use the app, and never in the background. Your coordinates never leave your phone.

One network request

When you type a city name, only that text is sent to a map service (OpenStreetMap Nominatim) to find its coordinates. Nothing else is sent.

Your history, tasbih counts and settings stay on your phone. You can export a copy any time from Settings.

Help

Quick answers

The times differ from my mosque. What do I do?

Open Settings and check the calculation method and the Asr rule. You can also add or take away minutes for each prayer, so Noor matches your mosque's timetable.

Does Noor work without internet?

Yes. Prayer times, Qibla, the calendar and every dua work offline. Only searching for a new city needs a connection.

Why does Noor need my location?

To work out prayer times and the Qibla for where you are. It uses your location only while the app is open and it never leaves your phone. You can also pick a city by hand.

Will my prayer alert arrive on time?

Noor schedules alerts on your phone and re-arms them after a restart, an update or a change of time zone. A health screen shows your permission, exact-alarm and battery status, and a test button sends you an alert right now.

Where do the dua come from?

From the Quran and from authentic hadith. Each dua shows its source, such as the surah and verse or the hadith collection.

Do I have to pay to use Noor?

No. Prayer times, alerts, Qibla and the calendar are free, and so is the General dua theme. Pro is a single payment that adds more dua themes and Ramadan mode.
